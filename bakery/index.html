What's inside

A cozy little story

Nana Nutmeg, the old baker cat, hands you the keys to a little autumn bakery. Walk Biscuit the cat around the island, collect wheat from the hamsters and bake it into treats your customers love.

🐹Helpful hamstersHamsters carry wheat from the fields and nap on the straw when the sack is full.
🎂Bread to fancy cakesBuild the Flour Mill, Cake Oven and Icing Table to bake finer treats.
🦊Cozy customersRabbits, hedgehogs, foxes and owls in knitted scarves. Nobody ever leaves angry.
🎃Pumpkin manorGrow your pumpkin cottage into the Golden Pumpkin Manor.
